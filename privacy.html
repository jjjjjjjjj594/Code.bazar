Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Disclaimer: Yeh ek general-purpose template hai, kisi wakeel ne review nahi kiya hai. Agar aap isse ek real business ke liye use kar rahe hain (especially agar aap EU/India ke data-protection laws ke daayre me aate hain), kisi legal professional se review karwana behtar rahega.

1. Hum kya data collect karte hain

DataKab collect hota haiKahan store hota hai
Naam, phone/emailJab aap "Buy" click karke WhatsApp/email par contact karte hainWhatsApp / Email — humare paas seedha nahi aata
Wishlist, cart-jaisi preferencesJab aap heart icon ya filters use karte hainSirf aapke browser ke localStorage me (humare server par nahi jaata — hamara koi server hi nahi hai)
Analytics (page views, clicks)Jab aap site browse karte hainAgar Google Analytics configured hai, to Google ke paas; nahi to sirf aapke browser me

2. Yeh site kaise kaam karti hai

CodeBazaar ek static website hai — koi backend/database nahi hai. Matlab:

  • Aapki wishlist, cart, aur browsing preferences sirf aapke apne browser me save hoti hain — kisi central server par nahi bheji jaati
  • Payment process manually UPI + WhatsApp se hoti hai — koi card/payment detail is website ke through collect nahi hoti
  • Jab aap "Buy" ya chat button click karte hain, aap WhatsApp (Meta) ya aapki email app kholte hain — us point ke baad aapki information un platforms ki privacy policy ke daayre me aati hai

3. Third-party services

Depending on kya configure kiya gaya hai, yeh site in services ko use kar sakti hai:

  • WhatsApp (Meta) — order/support conversations ke liye
  • Google Analytics — agar owner ne apna Measurement ID configure kiya hai, anonymous traffic patterns dekhne ke liye
  • EmailJS — agar configured hai, order notification emails bhejne ke liye

Yeh sab third-party services apni khud ki privacy policies follow karti hain.

4. Aapke rights

Chunki zyada tar data sirf aapke apne browser me hota hai, aap:

  • Apni wishlist/preferences kabhi bhi apne browser settings se clear kar sakte hain
  • WhatsApp/email par bheji gayi apni information delete/edit karne ke liye humein directly contact kar sakte hain

5. Contact

Privacy se related kisi bhi sawaal ke liye WhatsApp ke through contact karein — link site ke header aur chat widget me diya gaya hai.
